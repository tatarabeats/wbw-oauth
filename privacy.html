Privacy Policy

WannaBeWise Publisher is a personal tool used by a single creator to publish their own short videos to their own TikTok account.

Data we access

The app requests only the permissions needed to upload videos to the account owner's own TikTok account (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data of any other user.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are never transmitted to any third party.
